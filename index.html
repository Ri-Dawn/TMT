Before You Ask

A few things worth knowing.

Is tarot fortune-telling — will you tell me exactly what happens next?+
No, and be cautious of anyone who claims they can. Tarot doesn't hand you a locked script of the future — it reads the energy and patterns already in motion around your question, and shows you where they're heading if nothing changes. That's not a hedge. It's genuinely more useful: you leave seeing your present with total clarity, not gambling on a prophecy.
Isn't this just vague statements that could apply to anyone?+
Fair instinct — a lot of what passes for tarot online is exactly that. The difference is specificity. Your cards are read live, against the real question you bring, not recycled into something generic enough to fit anyone's Tuesday. If a reading could apply to a stranger just as easily as you, it wasn't really a reading.
Do I need to believe in tarot for it to work?+
No. Some of the most direct sessions we've given were for people who came in openly skeptical. What's actually happening is intuition and symbolism reading the real energy around a specific question — and that works whether or not you've decided to believe in it yet.
Can a reading predict something bad? Should I be scared to ask?+
Cards that look dark at a glance almost never mean what pop culture trained you to fear. They usually point at change, an ending, or something asking for your attention — not a countdown to disaster. If something difficult surfaces, you'll also hear what to do with it. We don't deliver a warning and leave you there.
I've had a reading before that didn't feel accurate. Why would this be different?+
Usually one of two things happened: the question was too broad for the cards to land on anything specific, or the reader worked from a script instead of your actual situation. We spend the first minute narrowing your question until it's sharp enough to actually answer — that's most of what accuracy comes down to, and it's on us to get right, not you.
Which is better — voice call, Instagram call, or messages?+
Voice and Instagram calls give you the reading in real time, with room for follow-up. Both support video if you'd like your cards drawn live in front of you. Messages suit seekers who'd rather sit with their reading in writing, at their own pace. The depth is identical either way.
Is my session confidential?+
Always. Nothing from your session is shared. Anonymised, unrecognisable moments occasionally appear in our content, only ever with explicit permission.
What if I need to reschedule?+
Reach out as early as you can and your slot moves to the next available hour — no charge, so long as it's ahead of the original time.
